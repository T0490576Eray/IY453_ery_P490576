



**FOOTBALL CAREER JOURNEY**

------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ 

- INTRODUCTION
  
  In this project  I am devoloping text-based "choose your own adventure" game using c++.The story is about young football player who wants to become a professional football player. Player will make important decisions about training,personal life and matches.The purpose of this software to creating interactive game with using text,choices and simple logic.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

- ANALYSIS & DESIGN
  
  **First Stage**
  
  Choices: There are at least 30 binary choices some choices lead the victory while others lead to game over.
  
  Inventory system: Player can collect item like "professional football boots"" to increase shoot accuracy,"energy drink"" to restore health,"needle" to recovery from injury 
  
  Player Statistics: System tracks energy,attack and score to determine if the player can continue or faces a game over.
  
  **Second Stage**
  
  Inputs: Player name , Players kit number, Player choices
  
  Outputs: Story messages, Result of choices, Game over messages, Inventory List,Error or invalid input messages,Final screen
  
  Processes: Show story text,check player choice,add or remove items from inventory
